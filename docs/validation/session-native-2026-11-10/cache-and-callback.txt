Cache scheduling:
- Once all requested pages are ready, each enabled reader blocks on a semaphore rather than waking every 20 ms.
- Callback prefetch signals only when the requested 4096-frame page changes. An atomic pending bit coalesces notifications to at most one queued semaphore post.
- The callback never waits on a semaphore. Control pause/close wakes the reader; paused readers park on the existing condition variable. EINTR is handled on the reader.
- The production Engine host test waits for a cache-ready parked reader, verifies 40 ms with unchanged read/wakeup counters, verifies same-page callbacks do not wake it, exercises a callback page transition under allocation/mutex/fileIO instrumentation, then checks explicit page-cross refill, paused seek, resume, and concurrent legacy/session shutdown.

Composer scheduling:
- External timeline beat is still supplied per sample.
- Voice-command draining, plan adoption, settings and solo scans run once per callback.
- Backing all-notes-off runs on transport/seek transitions instead of every paused sample.
